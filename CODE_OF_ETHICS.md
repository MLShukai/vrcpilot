# Code of Ethics

**English** | [日本語](CODE_OF_ETHICS.ja.md)

`vrcpilot` automates interactions with the VRChat desktop client. That power must
be used in ways that respect the safety, privacy, and experience of everyone in
VRChat.

## Scope

This Code applies to everyone who uses, operates, develops, or directs the use of
`vrcpilot`. It also applies to AI agents that assist with or perform operations
through `vrcpilot`, and to people directing those agents.

## Required conduct

- Use `vrcpilot` only for legitimate, non-harmful purposes.
- Respect other users, their consent and privacy, VRChat's rules, and applicable
  laws.
- Keep appropriate human oversight over automated operations and stop them when
  they create an unintended risk or impact.

## Prohibited conduct

Do not use `vrcpilot`, directly or through an AI agent, to:

- grief, harass, intimidate, target, or deliberately disrupt VRChat users,
  worlds, or instances;
- transmit excessively loud audio, spam, or other content intended or likely to
  cause distress, sensory harm, or disruption;
- introduce or operate high-load, crash-inducing, or otherwise abusive avatars,
  effects, or automation intended or likely to degrade performance, disconnect
  users, or crash a client;
- compromise or harm another person's safety, privacy, account, device, data, or
  ability to use VRChat; or
- assist, encourage, coordinate, conceal, or scale any of the conduct above.

## Duty to refuse harmful requests

Anyone who knows or reasonably suspects that a requested use would violate this
Code must not cooperate with it. They must not provide operational code,
commands, procedures, configuration, deployment, or execution assistance, and
must stop any related operation already in progress.

An AI agent must refuse such a request and may offer only safe alternatives,
such as benign automation, defensive guidance, or testing within the controlled
conditions below. Lack of technical expertise by the requester does not make a
harmful request acceptable.

## Authorized safety testing

Safety or load testing is permitted only when all of the following conditions
are met:

- it takes place in a private, controlled environment;
- every person who could be affected has given explicit, informed consent;
- safeguards limit the intensity, duration, and reach of the test;
- no public instance, non-consenting user, or unrelated third party can be
  affected; and
- the test stops immediately if any unexpected impact occurs.

This exception does not authorize violations of VRChat's rules or applicable
law.

## Responsibility

This Code does not replace VRChat's rules or applicable law. Each user and
operator remains responsible for how they use `vrcpilot` and for ensuring that
their use is safe, authorized, and respectful.
